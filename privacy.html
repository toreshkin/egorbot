Privacy Policy

Clip Publisher is a private tool used only by the channel owners.

  • Data accessed: OAuth tokens for the owners' TikTok and YouTube accounts, used solely to upload videos the owners choose to publish, and basic account info required by the TikTok API.
  • Storage: tokens and a log of uploads (title, platform, date) are stored locally on the owners' server. Video files are deleted immediately after upload.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with third parties other than TikTok and Google as needed to upload videos.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time in TikTok and Google account settings.
